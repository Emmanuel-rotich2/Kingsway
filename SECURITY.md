# Private security reporting policy

Kingsway Preparatory School treats the security of this privately owned system
and the privacy of its school community as critical. The system may process
information concerning children, parents/guardians, staff, authentication,
finances, admissions, health, discipline, and school operations.

The system was built and is maintained by **Angisoft Technologies**, which is
the authorized technical and security contact for the project.

## Reporting a concern

Report suspected vulnerabilities or exposure **privately to Angisoft
Technologies through its official authorized channels**.

Do not:

- create a public GitHub issue, discussion, pull request, advisory, post, or
  proof of concept;
- disclose repository contents, architecture, routes, database information,
  credentials, screenshots, logs, school records, or personal information;
- test production, access accounts, scan infrastructure, extract data, alter
  records, contact users, or attempt exploitation without explicit written
  authorization;
- upload evidence to public or third-party services not approved by the school.

Provide only the minimum information needed through the approved private
channel. Preserve evidence securely and do not retain school data beyond the
authorized incident-handling period.

## Authorization

No permission to perform security testing is granted by repository visibility
or by this policy. Testing requires explicit written scope, approved systems,
approved dates, and named authorization from Kingsway Preparatory School.

Angisoft Technologies will coordinate technical validation, containment, and
remediation with Kingsway Preparatory School. The school retains ownership and
authority over school decisions, notifications, and legally required reporting.

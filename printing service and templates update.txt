5. BULK CHUNKING
============================================================

Large jobs must be chunked to avoid Dompdf memory exhaustion.

Defaults:

chunk_size = 100
maximum chunk_size = 200
minimum chunk_size = 1

Example:

400 selected students
→ 4 PDF files
→ 100 students per PDF

For both-side A4 output:

100 students
÷ 3 students per page
≈ 34 pages per PDF

The backend response should include:

{
    "printer_mode": "a4_pdf",
    "batch_mode": "bulk",
    "side": "both",
    "cards_per_a4_page": 3,
    "total_cards": 400,
    "total_chunks": 4,
    "chunk_size": 100,
    "estimated_pages": 134,
    "files": [
        "...001.pdf",
        "...002.pdf",
        "...003.pdf",
        "...004.pdf"
    ]
}
